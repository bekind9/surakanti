Mission

Careful support for capable people.

Many capable people in villages have strong business ideas but limited access to capital, mentorship, or digital support. Surakanti Ventures exists to run small, careful experiments that help practical local businesses get started or grow.

How it works

Simple steps, clear follow-up.

Each request is reviewed carefully. Support is matched to the real need, not to a fixed program or promise.

Apply or get referred

Share the person, village, business idea, and the kind of help needed.

Review the need

We look at the business idea, local demand, use of support, and follow-up plan.

Support the work

Support may include small funding, tools, equipment, guidance, or digital help.

Follow up and learn

We check progress, learn what worked, and share lessons transparently over time.
