Surakanti Ventures

Empowering village businesses with capital, tools, and simple technology.

Our mission is simple: help capable people in villages start or grow practical small businesses with modest support, digital tools, and AI-enabled guidance.

We bring useful technology closer to local entrepreneurs: phone records, digital payments, basic online presence, simple planning help, and the right tools at the right time.
